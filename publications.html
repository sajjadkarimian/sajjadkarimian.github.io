Publications

Journal articles, conference papers and preprints on health informatics, user-centred system design and trust in healthcare AI. Full list on Google Scholar and ORCID.

Journal articles

  1. Trustworthy artificial intelligence and organisational trust: a scoping review using socio-technical systems analysis

    Ergonomics, 2025

    Looks at when AI earns or loses trust inside healthcare organisations, using socio-technical systems analysis to cover the human, structural and contextual factors that decide whether AI is adopted and used safely.

    Read the paper: Trustworthy artificial intelligence and organisational trust: a scoping review using socio-technical systems analysis

  2. Requirements specification, design, and evaluation of dental image exchange and management system with user-centered approach

    Health Science Reports, 2023

    A case study of the full delivery loop: gathering requirements, designing and evaluating a dental image management system with clinical users at the centre.

    Read the paper: Requirements specification, design, and evaluation of dental image exchange and management system with user-centered approach

  3. A systematic review of effects of exchanging and sharing medical image systems in a sociotechnical context

    Informatics in Medicine Unlocked, 2023

    Reviews the organisational workflows, user behaviour and governance structures that decide whether medical image exchange systems work in practice.

    Read the paper: A systematic review of effects of exchanging and sharing medical image systems in a sociotechnical context

  4. Colorectal cancer risk factors in north-eastern Iran: a retrospective cross-sectional study based on GIS and spatial analysis

    Geospatial Health, 2019

    Maps colorectal cancer risk factors with GIS methods to show spatial patterns that aggregate statistics hide.

Conference papers and preprints

  1. Integrating Human Factors Socio-Technical Systems Analysis in a Data Investigation in an Acute Hospital

    Healthcare Ergonomics and Patient Safety (HEPS), 2025

    Draws on fieldwork at St James's Hospital to show how human factors and socio-technical analysis can be applied to a clinical data investigation.

  2. Measuring the Organizational Trust of Artificial Intelligence Systems in Healthcare with Socio-Technical Systems Analysis Methods

    Irish Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Annual Conference

    Proposes a structured way to measure whether an organisation trusts a healthcare AI system enough to use it safely and consistently.

  3. Imagining better AI-enabled healthcare futures: the case for care by design

    arXiv preprint, 2024

    Argues that better AI-enabled healthcare depends on design choices, and examines how values built into AI systems shape clinical care.

    Read the preprint: Imagining better AI-enabled healthcare futures: the case for care by design

  4. Integrating Human Factors into Trustworthy AI for Healthcare

    Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence Workshop, 2023

    Argues that human factors analysis of clinical work systems belongs in any rigorous AI evaluation and governance framework.

  5. A Systematic Review for Evaluating the Effects of Teleradiology

    Iranian Journal of Radiology, 2019

    Reviews how remote radiology services affect clinical quality, efficiency and patient safety.
