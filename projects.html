UCD School of Medicine · Malawi · 2026

Dental image exchange and management system

MSc thesis · Urmia, Iran · 2017 – 2020

My role: requirements, design specifications, focus groups, user acceptance testing and evaluation.

Problem
Dental radiology images in Urmia's private sector were exchanged on film and paper. Patients carried images between radiology centres and dentists, and images were lost or damaged.
Stakeholders
Dentists, radiologists, radiology assistants and patients.
Elicitation
Semi-structured interviews and observation of how dentists order and review radiology exams. Requirements were ranked by how often users raised them.
Top requirements
Ease of use and a user-friendly interface first, followed by speed, security, communication, notifications and a mobile app.
Design
Use case diagrams and an entity-relationship diagram (ERD). Development was outsourced to a developer, working from these specifications.
Validation
A 10-person focus group reviewed the prototype over several rounds until users were satisfied. I then ran user acceptance testing and a formative evaluation in the first month of live use, followed by a questionnaire with 40 respondents.
Result
In the first month the system was used by 4 radiology centres, 9 dentists and 50 patients. 52.5% of users accessed it on a computer and 40% on a mobile phone. Users reported central image storage, fewer lost or damaged images and less radiation exposure for patients.

Clinical data flow and reporting analysis

St James's Hospital · 2024 – 2025
Problem

What I did
Elicited requirements from clinical and operational staff, mapped workflows and data flows across departments, and documented reporting standards.
Result
Workflow maps and structured outputs that showed where data quality broke down, used by senior clinical and operational stakeholders. Presented at HEPS 2025.
